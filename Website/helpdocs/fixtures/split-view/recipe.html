Lemon tart

A crisp, buttery shell filled with a sharp, silky lemon curd. Make the pastry the day before and the tart comes together in under an hour.

Ingredients

Measuring in cups? Keep the kitchen conversions open beside the recipe.

  • 200 g plain flour
  • 100 g cold butter
  • 4 lemons, zest and juice
  • 4 eggs and 150 g sugar

Method

Blind-bake the shell at 180 °C for 20 minutes, pour in the curd and bake for 25 minutes more, until just set with a slight wobble.
