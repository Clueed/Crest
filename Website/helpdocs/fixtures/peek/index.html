Trail Notes

Three ridge walks for a long weekend

Each of these walks starts from a village with a bus stop and ends somewhere with a view worth the climb. None needs more than a day, and all of them are quiet outside the summer holidays.

Before you go, read the mountain weather forecast. Ridges turn cold and windy long before the valleys do, and the last stretch of each walk has no shelter.

Pack light

Bring water, a warm layer and a map. The paths are marked, but cloud can come down quickly on the tops.
